Discover New York

Destination Overview

New York is a place where relentless ambition meets breathtaking natural beauty. Stretching from the world-famous, neon-lit streets of its coastal metropolis to the expansive wilderness of the northern border, the state offers an unmatched contrast of towering skyscrapers, glacial lakes, roaring waterfalls, and serene mountain ranges.

Every region has its own character. Catch a world-class Broadway show in Manhattan, hike the historic trails of the majestic Adirondacks, tour the award-winning cool-climate wineries of the Finger Lakes, or feel the powerful mist of Niagara Falls. A deep-rooted history, trailblazing cultural scenes, and an unmistakable, diverse energy make every journey unforgettable.

Whether you're planning a fast-paced urban exploration, an adventurous mountain road trip, or a relaxing lakeside getaway, New York offers experiences that combine sophisticated art and culture with wild, untouched landscapes throughout every season.

Your Journey

Explore New York One Landmark At A Time

Follow a carefully crafted route through the city's most iconic attractions.

01 Bright lights start

Times Square

Step into the glow of Broadway screens, street performers, and the nonstop rhythm that makes Midtown feel alive at every hour.

02 Skyline climb

Empire State Building

Ride upward for sweeping city views, from the Hudson River to the bridges, rooftops, and avenues stretching across Manhattan.

03 Green pause

Central Park

Slow the pace with tree-lined paths, lake views, quiet lawns, and the calm side of New York tucked between towering streets.

04 City glow

Rockefeller Center

Stop by the plaza for Art Deco details, seasonal energy, and a classic Midtown view surrounded by shops and towers.

05 Grand entrance

Grand Central Terminal

Walk beneath the famous celestial ceiling and feel the rush of a landmark that still moves New York every day.

06 Art and avenues

The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Explore one of the world's greatest museums before stepping back out toward Fifth Avenue and the edge of Central Park.

07 Elevated walk

The High Line

Follow gardens, public art, and city views along a raised railway path that turns the West Side into a slow scenic walk.

08 Downtown view

One World Observatory

Head downtown for a powerful skyline moment, with glassy views over the harbor, bridges, and Lower Manhattan.

09 Harbor story

Statue of Liberty

Cross toward the harbor for the city's most symbolic welcome, with ferry views that frame Manhattan from the water.

10 Golden hour finish

Brooklyn Bridge

End the route above the East River, where the bridge cables, city lights, and Brooklyn skyline make the perfect final chapter.

Deep Dive

Must Visit Attractions

Explore the detailed guide to New York's most iconic destinations

Empire State Building

  • • 102-floor skyscraper
  • • Observation deck with 360° views
  • • Best sunset views in Manhattan
  • • Art Deco architectural masterpiece

Experience iconic views from one of the world's most recognizable landmarks. The observation decks offer breathtaking panoramas of the city skyline, Central Park, and beyond.

Rockefeller Center

  • • Top of the Rock observation deck
  • • Ice skating rink (winter season)
  • • Shopping and dining complex
  • • City panorama views

A bustling plaza filled with Art Deco architecture, seasonal energy, and one of the best elevated city views. Perfect for shopping, dining, and seasonal activities.

Central Park

  • • 843 acres of urban greenery
  • • Tree-lined paths and scenic lakes
  • • Museums and cultural venues
  • • Peaceful escape from the city

Discover New York's lungs - a massive park offering peaceful walks, scenic views, cultural attractions, and recreational spaces right in the heart of Manhattan.

Statue of Liberty

  • • Iconic symbol of freedom
  • • Crown access available
  • • Harbor and city views
  • • UNESCO World Heritage Site

Cross the harbor to experience America's most recognizable symbol. The ferry views of Manhattan skyline combined with the statue create an unforgettable moment.
