Before you book

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know a tour's total price?

Each tour card and detail page shows a clear starting price. Any add-ons are shown before you confirm a booking.

Can I change my travel dates after booking?

Yes, most tours allow date changes. Reach out through the contact form and our team will confirm availability.

Do I need a visa for these destinations?

Visa requirements depend on your nationality and destination. We share the latest guidance on each tour's detail page.

What group sizes do you support?

From solo trips to full family groups. Use the group size filter to see tours that match your party.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation windows vary by tour and are listed clearly at checkout, along with any partial-refund terms.

How do payments work?

You can pay in full or reserve with a deposit, depending on the tour. Details are shown on the booking form.
